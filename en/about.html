About this guide

Created by Sasuke, a Paris-based wedding and couple photographer. This site is a separate project from that work — it's a practical guide, not a portfolio.

Who writes this

I live in Paris and photograph weddings and couple sessions here. Over years of scouting locations and working with visiting couples, I ended up with a lot of practical, repeatedly-useful knowledge about the city that has nothing to do with photography — which bakery actually won this year's baguette competition, which neighborhoods are walkable, what the tax-free paperwork actually involves. This guide is where that knowledge lives.

This site does not currently link to my photography business — that site isn't finished yet. If you're curious about a Paris couple photoshoot, that's a separate project for later.

How this guide is put together

Facts are sourced, not guessed

Prices, hours, transit routes, and competition results are checked against official sources (city of Paris, RATP, French customs, hotel and venue websites) or established travel and local media. Each page links to what it's based on. Anything that changes often — prices, hours, schedules — carries a "check before you travel" note rather than being stated as permanent.

Hotels are chosen by location first

Hotel picks prioritize walkability, safety, and proximity to what you're actually there to see — especially for first-time visitors and couples. A hotel with beautiful interiors in an inconvenient location doesn't make the list unless the hotel itself is the destination (a small number of "worth traveling for" picks are marked as such). See the hotels page for the full method.

Affiliate links, and what they don't influence

Some hotel and booking links on this site are affiliate links: if you book through one, this site may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. A hotel is never added, removed, or ranked higher because of a commission — the hotel list is decided first, based on location and guest experience, and affiliate links are added afterward where available. If no affiliate link exists yet for a hotel, no button is shown.

Questions or corrections? This guide is actively maintained — details that go out of date get fixed.
