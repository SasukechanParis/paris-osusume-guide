Paris's two- and three-star tables

The 2026 Michelin Guide lists 127 starred restaurants in Paris: 98 with one star, 20 with two, and 9 with three. This page covers just the top tier — the 29 two- and three-star restaurants — since a flat list of 100+ names isn't actually that useful on its own. For each one you get the facts that don't need a food critic to verify: arrondissement, address, and a map link, sourced back to where the star count was reported.

Looking for one-star restaurants?

Paris also has around 100 one-star restaurants in the current guide — enough that a flat list stops being useful and starts being noise. The Michelin Guide's own site is the best place to browse them, since it stays current and lets you filter by cuisine and price.

Star ratings and the restaurant count are sourced from the 2026 Michelin Guide as reported by French media, with each entry linking to its source. Star ratings, and even whether a restaurant is still open, can change with each year's guide — always confirm directly with the restaurant or the Michelin Guide's own site before booking.
